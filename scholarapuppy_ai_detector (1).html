5-Pillar Mathematical AI Detection Engine

How ScholaraPuppy calculates sub-scores while protecting scientific lab reports

1. Mathematical Burstiness (25% Weight)

Counts words in every sentence and computes the mathematical Standard Deviation ($\sigma$). Humans mix 5-word and 35-word sentences ($\sigma \ge 8.0$). AI clusters tightly around 15–22 words ($\sigma < 4.5$), triggering a high burstiness penalty.

2. Cliché & Syntax Regex Penalty (25% Weight)

Applies weighted regex penalties to LLM transition habits and stock phrases ("It is important to note," "Crucially," "Furthermore," "Delve," "A testament to," "In conclusion," "Vital") normalized per 100 words.

3. STEM Jargon Whitelist & Glue Density (20% Weight)

Strips out whitelisted Biology, Chemistry, Medical, and CS terms (e.g., spectrophotometry, molarity, centrifugation, polymerase, titration) before measuring lexical predictability—saving human lab reports from false flags.

4. Paragraph Symmetry Variance (15% Weight)

Measures character lengths across paragraphs. If body paragraphs have near-zero variance (e.g., 400 chars, 405 chars, 395 chars; Coefficient of Variation $< 0.12$), the symmetry detector increases the AI probability.

5. Flesch-Kincaid Readability + Live Grammar API (15% Weight)

Calculates the exact Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level ($0.39 \times \text{ASL} + 11.8 \times \text{ASW} - 15.59$) and queries the public LanguageTool Grammar API (with local heuristic fallback). When a text reads at a dense post-graduate level ($\text{FK} \ge 13.5$) with zero grammatical, punctuation, or typographical asymmetries, it flags synthetic perfection.
